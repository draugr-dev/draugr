A rule in the Saga is written as the value on its own again: `exposure: public`, `failOnPriority: P1`, `deny: ["AGPL-3.0-only"]`. The `{value, reason}` shape introduced in 0.107.0 is gone. It made every descriptor longer to enable something almost nobody wrote — across the descriptors shipped with Draugr, nineteen rules gained a line each and one carried a reason. `config.exclude` is untouched and still requires a reason, which is the place a decision genuinely needs arguing for.
